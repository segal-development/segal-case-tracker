# Mero tramite / Apercibimiento poder y/o titulo also start the 3-day "acreditar poder" plazo (forward-only)
Rodrigo Campos (lawyer) and Carla Segal (jefa juridica) confirmed a rule; Carla approved the roll-out.

## Specs
- S1. The rule (Rodrigo, verbatim excerpts): "Todas las resoluciones senaladas, indistintamente de la terminologia se debe hacer una gestion por nuestra parte dentro de 3 dias por regla general, la cual, de no llevarse a cabo significa que se pierde lo presentado." The resolutions are `Mero tramite`, `Previo a proveer` and `Apercibimiento poder y/o titulo` ("es el mismo efecto del mero tramite, pero con otra terminologia"), dictated by the court AFTER our `Opone excepciones` escrito and BEFORE the court moves to `Opone excepciones-Traslado`. "Si no se cumple la diligencia dentro de 3 dias desde que se dicta, conlleva a perder la defensa" (red). Counter-example (C-1319-2026): the court went straight to `Opone excepciones-Traslado` with no such resolution: no obligation, the defense is not lost. Carla's decision (verbatim): "La activamos solo para resoluciones nuevas desde hoy".
- S2. Today only `Previo a proveer` creates the plazo `acreditar_poder_3d` (3 business days, Saturday counts, fatal). Logic: app/services/poder_deadline.py (`find_poder_obligation`, `is_compliance_movement`, `_TRIGGER_RE`, the "whose obligation is it" heuristic around `is_excepciones_filing`), the upsert in app/services/deadline_engine.py (look for "Upsert ACREDITAR_PODER_3D", ~line 1164), the verdict in app/services/deadline_verdict.py (`_detect_poder`) and app/services/sync_service.py (~2615-2680). Read all of them first.
- S3. Extend the TRIGGER: besides `Previo a proveer` (unchanged, all history, exactly as today), a court act (`procedure` is not `Escrito`; same `_is_court_act` rule), not `[Nulo]`, whose description starts with `Mero tramite` (accent-insensitive: "Mero trámite") or `Apercibimiento poder` starts the plazo ONLY IF all hold: (a) its `movement_date` >= the cutoff setting (S4); (b) there is an `Opone excepciones` escrito (the existing `is_excepciones_filing`) on or before it; (c) no `Opone excepciones-Traslado` resolution exists on or before it (same day counts as "the court already moved on": no obligation). The existing side/ownership heuristic (the firm is on the ejecutado's side and an excepciones filing precedes) applies to the new triggers exactly as to `Previo a proveer`. When several triggers exist, keep the existing "latest trigger wins" behaviour.
- S4. Setting `PODER_EXTENDED_FROM` in app/config.py: ISO date string, default `"2026-11-04"` (today, Carla's "desde hoy"); empty string = the extension is OFF (only `Previo a proveer`, as today). Parse once, safely (an invalid value = OFF + a WARNING at import/first use, never a crash).
- S5. Compliance (closes the plazo as `cumplido`): the existing wording (`Acredita Poder`, `Poder acreditado`, `Patrocinio y poder`, `Delega poder`) dated strictly after the trigger, unchanged for `Previo a proveer`. For the NEW triggers also accept `Cumple lo ordenado`. Also, for the NEW triggers, if an `Opone excepciones-Traslado` resolution is dated after the trigger, the court moved on: the defense was not lost, so the plazo must NOT read as an unfulfilled miss (use the engine's existing way to close or supersede a plazo when the procedure moves on; read how step 5/6b treat superseded and expired rows and do not invent a new status). Do not change how `Previo a proveer` behaves in this respect.
- S6. Anchoring and counting are the existing ones (`anchor_date` falls back to `movement_date`; `add_business_days(day, 3)` as for the existing plazo). Fatal like the existing one. Same semaforo effect (rojo when due or past due and unfulfilled).
- S7. Strict TDD, tests first, RED seen, mirroring the existing poder tests (grep tests/ for `acreditar_poder`, `poder_deadline`, `Previo a proveer`): the cases of Rodrigo as scenarios: (1) C-10700 shape: Opone excepciones 21/09, Mero tramite dictated on or after the cutoff, `Acredita Poder` 2 days later -> plazo created, `cumplido`, no rojo; (2) same but no compliance and past due -> rojo / expired; (3) C-1319 shape: Opone excepciones then Opone excepciones-Traslado, no resolution -> no plazo; (4) Mero tramite BEFORE the cutoff -> no new plazo (and `Previo a proveer` before the cutoff still works); (5) Mero tramite after the Traslado resolution (Impugnacion de Sentencia stage) -> no plazo; (6) `Apercibimiento poder y/o titulo` in `Inicio de la Tramitacion` before any Opone excepciones -> no plazo; (7) extension OFF (empty setting) -> behaves as today; (8) `[Nulo]` movement ignored; (9) the court moves to Traslado after the Mero tramite -> not a miss; (10) the firm is on the plaintiff's side -> no plazo (existing heuristic). Existing tests keep passing untouched.
- S8. Out of scope: showing the resolutions in the case detail screen (a separate task), the 310 historical cases, any data change, other deadline types, the frontend.
